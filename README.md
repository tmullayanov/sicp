# SICP

My re-read of SICP and re-doing of all the exercises many years later after the first read.

Every exercise is done via `Racket`, `scheme` or `sicp` languages are used.

`notes.md` for every chapter are my own personal notes scribbled in plain Russian as I am reading the Russian translation of the book.

Every chapter's artifacts are collected into it's own respective folder (atm).

All exercises are either `.rkt` or `.md` files depending on the matter of the exercise.
They are written by me in English with no AI assistance.

This project is dedicated to the beauty of a design process and power of human intelligence.
