Golden ratio is one of the roots of the following equation:

$x^2 = x + 1$

We have two ways of how to express x in that equation:

a. $ x = x^2 - 1 $

b. $ x = 1 + 1/x $

If we note right-hand side of both variants as f(x) and g(x) respectively,
we get the notion of fixed-point:

a. $x = f(x)$ where $f(x) = x^2 - 1$
b. $x = g(x)$ where $g(x) = 1 + 1/x$

Interestingly enough, trying to find the fixed-point with SICP alrogithm leads each option
to converge to it's own root of the initial equation


```sh
"chapter-01\\fixed-point.rkt"> (fixed-point (lambda (x) (/ (+ x (* x x) -1) 2)) 1.0)                                                                   
-0.6180248067864138
"chapter-01\\fixed-point.rkt"> (/ (- 1 (sqrt 5)) 2)                                                                                                    
-0.618033988749989

"chapter-01\\fixed-point.rkt"> (fixed-point (lambda (x) (+ 1 (/ 1 x))) 1.0)                                                                            
1.6180555555555556
```
