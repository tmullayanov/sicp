# Proof of Fib(n)

**Proposition 1**: Fib(n) is the closest integer to $\phi^n / \sqrt{5}$
where $\phi$ is the solution to golden ratio and equals to $\dfrac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$


The hint: introduce $\psi = \dfrac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2}$, use the definition of Fib, and then apply the math.induction to prove that Fib(n) = $\dfrac{\phi^n - \psi^n}{\sqrt{5}}$

## 0. Intuition.

Both $\psi$ and $\phi$ are the roots of the following equation:

$x^2 = x + 1$ (*)


## 1. The base case

Let us use the formula directly to prove the base case.

fib(0) = $\dfrac{1 - 1}{\sqrt{5}} = 0$, trivial.

fib(1) = $\dfrac{(1+\sqrt{5}) - (1 - \sqrt{5})}{2\sqrt{5}}$ = 1, trivial.


## 2. Inductive hypothesis

Let's assume the following is correct for some arbitrary N and N-1:

$Fib(n) = \dfrac{\phi^n - \psi^n}{\sqrt{5}}$

$Fib(n-1) = \dfrac{\phi^{n-1} - \psi^{n-1}}{\sqrt{5}}$


## 3. Advance to the N+1

By the recursive definition, Fib(n+1) = Fib(n) + Fib(n-1).

$$
\begin{aligned}
Fib(n) + Fib(n-1) 
    &= \dfrac{\phi^n - \psi^n + \phi^{n-1} - \psi^{n-1}}{\sqrt{5}} \\
    &= \dfrac{\phi^{n-1} \cdot (\phi + 1)  - \psi^{n-1} \cdot (\psi + 1)}{\sqrt{5}} \\
    &\rlap{\text{Now, using (*) equation, we get this}} \\
    &= \dfrac{\phi^{n-1} \cdot \phi^2 - \psi^{n-1} 
    \cdot \psi^2}{\sqrt{5}} \\
    &= \dfrac{\phi^{n+1} - \psi^{n+1}}{\sqrt{5}}
\end{aligned}
$$

Which is exactly as it is supposed to be for $Fib(n+1)$ - the same formula with the power of $n+1$.

## 4. Advancing from Binet's formula to the main statement.

It is established that $Fib(n) = \dfrac{\phi^n - \psi^n}{\sqrt{5}}$, where $\phi$ and $\psi$ defined as above.

We need to prove that Fib(n) is the closest integer to $\dfrac{\phi^n}{\sqrt{5}}$.

Let's substract one from the other and estimate the difference:

$$
\begin{aligned}
|\dfrac{\phi^n}{\sqrt{5}} - Fib(n)| = |\dfrac{\psi^n}{\sqrt{5}}|
\end{aligned}
$$

For **Proposition 1** to be true, this difference must be less than 1/2 to round to the closest integer.

Let's check the assumption that $|\dfrac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2}| \leq 1$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\sqrt{5} - 1) / 2 < 1 \\
(\sqrt{5} - 1) < 2 \\
\sqrt{5} < 3 \\
5 < 9
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus, $|\psi| < 1$.
Hence, $|\psi^n|$ will also be less than 1.

Then $\dfrac{|\psi^n|}{\sqrt{5}} \leq \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{5}} < \dfrac{1}{2}$

Since the difference between terms $Fib(n)$ and $\dfrac{\phi^n}{\sqrt{5}}$ is less than $1/2$, and Fib(n) is integer by it's definition, it is indeed the closest integer to the $\dfrac{\phi^n}{\sqrt{5}}$.
